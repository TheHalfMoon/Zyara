# Zyara Network Competitor and Company Master Index

**Research date:** 2026-09-16
**Purpose:** give Astro a broad competitive universe for the full Zyara Network master plan.
**Rule:** this is a research index, not a ranking. Astro must reverify current official product pages before making time-sensitive feature claims.

The goal is not to copy every company. The goal is to prevent blind spots across discovery, clinic operations, AI automation, communications, clinical workflow, revenue cycle, interoperability and patient health continuity.
