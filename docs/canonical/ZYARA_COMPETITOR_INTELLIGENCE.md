# Zyara competitor intelligence

Research date: 2026-09-13. Product/research owner. 44 product/platform entries researched: 43 current public-product/documentation reviews plus one historical GCC screen. Docplanner, Doctoralia and TuoTempo are related products, not three independent corporate competitors. Vezeeta and Okadoc are counted once with Saudi scope.

## Method and limits

Fresh official product pages, help centers, API documentation, government sources and upstream repositories were read. No patient accounts, real bookings, clinic contracts or private dashboards were accessed. D = documented workflow/technical contract; M = advertised official feature; A = announced/beta availability not established; U = not established by this research, never proof of absence. Confidence describes the source claim, not deployed reliability. Rows record region/edition and URLs; every observation was checked on the date above. Vendor outcome statistics, user counts and integration counts are deliberately excluded as unverified performance evidence. Exact prices are not invented.

The [machine-readable full matrix](../research/competitor-feature-matrix.json) contains all 2684 competitor-feature cells, source references, observation state and Zyara adoption classification. The feature catalog defines a coherent treatment rather than copying the same feature differently merely because several competitors market it. An unknown cell still carries the proposed Zyara treatment; it is not an assertion that the competitor has that feature.

## Decision synthesis

Healthcare discovery, availability, reminders, portals, telehealth and even conversational AI are competitive expectations. The defensible hypothesis is a continuously corrected Saudi provider/service graph plus trustworthy cross-provider access workflows and attendance evidence. Hospital apps and national services already have records and family continuity; a shallow directory with an LLM would be weaker. There is no evidence here that any single feature is globally unique.

Prioritize clinic operations value over marketplace acquisition dependence. Borrow calendar interaction, accessible booking, forms and reminders. Improve honest request/confirmation, insurer specificity, expiry and reschedule safety. Differentiate through auditable authority/freshness, Arabic intent, transparent ranking and independently governed reviews. Defer clinical charting, payments, surgical optimization and autonomous clinical decisions. Reject commission-based booking economics, paid organic relevance, review gating and opaque no-show-based denial.

## Saudi operating implications and research gaps

Sehhaty and private hospital apps make family access, Arabic content and continuity baseline expectations. Almoosa's public request flow demonstrates why a booking-looking form must not be labeled instant. Insurance varies by branch/product, and NPHIES insurance onboarding is separate from scheduling. Public credential lookup is a verification aid, not licensed bulk provider data.

Before pilot, conduct authorized workflow interviews with reception, integration staff and patients at recruited clinics; observe request turnaround, cancellations, authority and attendance recording using synthetic cases. Request current edition/vendor capability documents and quotes where relevant. Unavailable public detail remains unknown; this plan does not claim private workflow validation. Measure actual patient preference against Google, Sehhaty, Vezeeta and hospital apps locally before claiming product-market fit.
